Gaming Areas & Hourly Rates

Tiered Battle Stations

At QazaqPeek, our hardware packages are split into distinct tiers tailored for different budgets and competitive intensity. Our Standard Hall provides high frame rates for everyday multiplayer sessions, while our VIP and Boot Camp suites feature tournament monitors running at 360Hz alongside sound-isolated booths.

All visitors pay through a unified guest account balance. Night packages guarantee a reserved desk from 22:00 until 08:00 the following morning. All rates are listed in Kazakhstani Tenge (KZT) and include full access to our pre-installed local game caching server.

How to Top Up Your Balance

  1. Approach the reception desk at the entrance or open the Kaspi terminal terminal app.
  2. Provide your registered mobile number or scan your membership QR code.
  3. Deposit cash or confirm the mobile transfer for your chosen hours.
  4. Take your seat, press the power button, and enter your login password.

Esports Club Glossary

Boot Camp
An isolated private room with five identically configured computers dedicated to professional team training and tournament qualifiers.
LAN Cache
A dedicated local server that updates major competitive games at gigabit speeds so guests never wait for client patches.
Night Package
A discounted flat-rate session granting continuous desk access from 22:00 until 08:00 the following morning.

Notice: Unused prepaid minutes roll over to your club balance and do not expire within 90 days.
