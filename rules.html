Esports & Venue Glossary

Tick Rate
The rate at which the server calculates physical updates per second. QazaqPeek match servers run at 128-tick for tournament consistency.
LAN Latency
Internal network response time between client machines and club routers, maintained below 1 millisecond.
DyAc+ Technology
BenQ's Dynamic Accuracy technology which minimizes screen motion blur during high-speed crosshair flicking in tactical shooters.
Bootcamp Room
A soundproof isolated 5-man team suite equipped with dedicated webcams and team comms for competitive practice.
