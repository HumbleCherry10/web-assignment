About the Venue

Located at Kenesary 69 in central Astana, QazaqPeek delivers an authentic competitive gaming experience across three dedicated zones: Standard (280Hz ASUS TUF), VIP 1 (310Hz ASUS TUF), and VIP 2 (360Hz Alienware). Every station is armed with 14th Gen Intel or AMD Ryzen 7 7800X3D processors, GeForce RTX 4060/5060/5060 Ti graphics, and premium esports peripherals including AULA F75 mechanical keyboards and HyperX Cloud II headsets.

We maintain strict room climate control at 19°C to prevent hardware thermal throttling, while sound-dampening partitions keep team voice comms clear. Whether running five-stack scrims in Counter-Strike 2 or grinding ranked matches in Dota 2, our network provides direct fiber connectivity with single-digit ping to Frankfurt and Stockholm servers.
