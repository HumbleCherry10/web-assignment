Professional Esports Environment on Kenesary Street

About the Venue

Located at Kenesary 69 in central Astana, QazaqPeek delivers an authentic competitive gaming experience for casual players and tournament teams alike. Our arena features machines configured with high-performance hardware and ultra-responsive 360Hz monitors, creating an optimal setup for tactical shooters and fast-paced competitive titles.

We maintain strict room climate control at 19°C to prevent hardware throttling, while sound-dampening partitions keep team voice comms clear. Whether running five-stack scrims in Counter-Strike 2 or grinding ranked matches in Dota 2, our network provides uninterrupted low-ping fiber-optic connectivity.

Every desk includes mechanical keyboards, precision optical sensors, and sanitized noise-isolating headsets. Refreshments and snacks are stocked continuously at the reception counter. We prioritize player ergonomics, providing adjustable lumbar-supported chairs alongside certified CAT-7 shielded cabling encased in H2O-resistant raceways.

We founded QazaqPeek on Kenesary to give local players a tournament-grade setup with stable ping and modern peripherals without compromises.

— Alikhan Nurpeisov, Shift Administrator (Interviewed on September 13, 2026)

Schedule & House Policy

Our club operates 24/7 every day of the week. Night package wristbands are issued starting promptly at 22:00. Guests under 18 years of age are admitted until 22:00 according to municipal laws.

Cleanliness is enforced at every battle station: outside drinks must have sealable, spill-proof caps.

Create a free club account to keep your booking details in one place, then visit the Events page for upcoming community nights and tournaments.
